Method. For each of the last 30 days a dynamic programme finds the cheapest charge and discharge plan across 48 half-hour slots, using that day's half-hourly Agile prices (Octopus Energy API: Agile Octopus for domestic, Shape Shifters Agile for business, both repriced every 30 minutes), the matching half-hourly export price, a synthetic load profile (household, office, retail or 24/7), and solar output estimated from Open-Meteo radiation (performance ratio 0.8). Each day starts and ends at 50% charge. Limits. It assumes perfect knowledge of the day's prices, uses a synthetic load, a winter-only window and no standing, network or demand charges (they do not change with the battery, but real business bills include them), so the annual figure is rough. A planning estimate, not financial advice.
